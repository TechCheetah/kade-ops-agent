# Travel & Expense Policy
Owner: Finance · Version 3.2 · Effective 1 March 2026

## Approval
Any business trip must be approved by the employee's line manager before booking. Trips outside the EU also need approval from the Finance Director.

## Transport
- Train is mandatory for destinations reachable in under 5 hours.
- Flights are economy class. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours.
- Taxis are reimbursed only between 22:00 and 06:00 or when carrying company equipment.

## Accommodation
The nightly hotel limit is €180 in Amsterdam, Paris and London, and €140 in all other cities.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance is €60. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Claims
Expense claims must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of the trip, with a receipt for every item above €25. Claims submitted later than 60 days are rejected.
