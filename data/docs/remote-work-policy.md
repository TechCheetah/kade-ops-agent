# Remote Work Policy
Owner: People & Culture · Version 2.0 · Effective 15 January 2026

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Operations staff in the Rotterdam warehouse are excluded because their work is on site.

Working from another country is allowed for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year and must be registered with HR at least 10 working days in advance, for tax and insurance reasons.

The company provides a one-off home office budget of €750 and a monthly internet allowance of €30.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 CET. Employees must be reachable on Slack during those hours.
