# Customs Clearance Procedure (Imports from outside the EU)
Owner: Customs Desk

1. The customs desk receives the commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading from the carrier.
2. Goods are classified with an HS code. Classification questions go to the senior customs officer.
3. The import declaration is filed in the customs filing system no later than 24 hours before arrival at Rotterdam.
4. Shipments with a declared value above €100,000 require a second review before filing.
5. Duties are paid through the deferred payment account and reconciled weekly by Finance.
