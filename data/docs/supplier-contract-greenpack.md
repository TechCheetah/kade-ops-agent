# Supplier Agreement — GreenPack Solutions B.V.
Contract ID: SUP-2026-003 · Signed 10 January 2026 · Term: 12 months

## Scope
Recycled cardboard packaging and pallet wrap for the Rotterdam warehouse.

## Payment terms
Invoices are payable within 30 days. A 2% early-payment discount applies when an invoice is paid within 10 days.

## Minimum order
Minimum monthly order value is €8,000. Price changes require 60 days' notice.

## Sustainability
GreenPack must provide a quarterly certificate showing at least 85% recycled content.

## Termination
Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice.
