# IT Incident Runbook
Owner: IT Operations · Version 4.1

## Severity levels
- **SEV1** — a core system (warehouse management, customs filing, or invoicing) is down for everyone. Response within 15 minutes, 24/7. The on-call engineer pages the IT Lead immediately.
- **SEV2** — a core system is degraded or down for one site. Response within 1 hour during business hours.
- **SEV3** — a single user or non-core tool is affected. Response within 1 business day.

## Escalation
SEV1 incidents are escalated to the CTO if not resolved within 2 hours. A post-mortem is mandatory for every SEV1 and must be published within 5 business days.

## Reporting
Incidents are reported through the helpdesk with department, a short title, a description of impact and a severity. Phone reports go to the on-call number only for SEV1.
