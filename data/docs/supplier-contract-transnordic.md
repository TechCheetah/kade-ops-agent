# Supplier Agreement — TransNordic Haulage A/S
Contract ID: SUP-2025-014 · Signed 2 June 2025 · Term: 24 months

## Scope
Road freight between the Rotterdam warehouse and distribution hubs in Hamburg, Copenhagen and Gothenburg.

## Payment terms
Invoices are payable within 45 days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest of 1% per month. Disputed invoices must be raised in writing within 14 days of receipt; the undisputed part must still be paid on time.

## Service levels
TransNordic guarantees 96% on-time delivery per calendar quarter. If on-time delivery falls below 96%, Kade Logistics receives a 5% credit on that quarter's invoices.

## Liability
Liability for lost or damaged goods is capped at €250,000 per incident.

## Termination
Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice. The contract renews automatically for 12 months unless terminated before 2 March 2027.
