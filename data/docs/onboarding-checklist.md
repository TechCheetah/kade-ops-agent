# New Employee Onboarding Checklist
Owner: People & Culture

## Before day 1
- HR sends the contract and collects ID and bank details.
- IT orders a laptop at least 5 working days before the start date.
- The manager assigns an onboarding buddy.

## Week 1
- Security awareness training (mandatory, must be completed within the first 5 days).
- Access to Slack, Google Workspace and the expense tool.
- Warehouse safety induction for anyone visiting the Rotterdam site.

## Month 1
- Meeting with the manager to set 90-day goals.
- Access to the warehouse management system is granted only after the safety induction.
